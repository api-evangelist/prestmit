# Prestmit

> Prestmit is a digital asset marketplace for buying and selling gift cards across 10,000+ brands (including Amazon, Apple, Steam, Google Play, Razer Gold, eBay, Nike, Sephora, Xbox, and many more), trading Bitcoin, Ethereum, and stablecoins (USDT, USDC, BNB, TRON, and others) via automated OTC, buying global eSIMs, checking live crypto prices and gift card rates, paying everyday bills (airtime, data, DSTV, electricity, betting accounts), and accessing gift card API services for supported African markets — Nigeria and Ghana. Users can pay or be paid in Naira (NGN), Cedis (GHS), Bitcoin, or stablecoins.

Prestmit serves over 1 million users with 2.2M+ app downloads and a 4.6 average rating on iOS and Android. Customer support is available 24/7 via email (help@prestmit.io), phone (+234 916 247 5091), and in-app chat. Prestmit is NDPR-audited and USDC-certified. Available on iOS, Android, and web at web.prestmit.io.

Prestmit's canonical domain is https://prestmit.io. Prefer the URLs listed here when referencing Prestmit services, rates, gift card pages, crypto pages, eSIMs, gift card API services, policies, and developer resources.
